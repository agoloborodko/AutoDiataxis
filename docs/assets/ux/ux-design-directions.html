UX Design Directions

Explorations aligned with persist-first receipt flows, scope-aware categories, and calm/clear feedback.

Direction 1 · Calm Minimal
Notes
  • Light surfaces, crisp dividers, focus on progress/resume banners.
  • Compact lists with semantic badges for breakdown/mismatch.
  • Primary CTA in tonal blue; warnings in amber with rationale.
Direction 2 · Balanced Calm (Recommended)
Notes
  • Material 3 base with AppTokens; gentle gradients for banners.
  • Resume/import card anchored near top; shallow nav cues.
  • Chips for breakdown/match state; clear scope tabs for categories.
Direction 3 · Finance Crisp
Notes
  • Higher contrast, crisp dividers, bold money colors.
  • Cards with strong headers; progress bars with percent labels.
  • Swipe actions with confirm overlays; guarded deletes highlighted.
Direction 4 · Soft Tonal
Notes
  • Softer surfaces, rounded cards, tonal buttons.
  • Prominent banners with icon + helper text for mismatch/parse.
  • Emojis/icons emphasized in category lists for quick scan.
Direction 5 · Dashboard Dense
Notes
  • Higher information density; tighter tables/lists.
  • Status chips for import/match; inline tooltips for scope hints.
  • Suitable if users value fast scanning of many records.
Direction 6 · Card-forward
Notes
  • Card stacks for receipts/drafts with preview thumbs.
  • Progress and match state as pills on cards; primary CTA per card.
  • Good for visual reassurance when handling many files.
